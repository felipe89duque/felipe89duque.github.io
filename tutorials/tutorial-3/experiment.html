Passive spread along an axon

Quick concepts

Passive voltage spread

1. Length constant

Voltage decays to 1/e of its initial change over one length constant.

2. Cable properties

Higher membrane resistance and larger diameter let voltage spread farther.

3. Passive delay

Capacitance changes how quickly voltage changes, but not the steady-state length constant.

4. Sealed ends

No axial current leaves the ends, so the voltage profile flattens at the boundaries.
