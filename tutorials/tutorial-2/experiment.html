Tutorial 2

Sodium action potential

Quick concepts

Action potential dynamics

1. Depolarization

Rapid influx of Na⁺ depolarizes the membrane toward ENa.

2. Repolarization

K⁺ outflow repolarizes the membrane back toward EK.

3. Refractory Period

Absolute: Na⁺ channel inactivation. Relative: K⁺ hyperpolarization.

Parameters

Adjust channel conductances or ion concentrations to observe how they affect the action potential waveform and refractory period.
