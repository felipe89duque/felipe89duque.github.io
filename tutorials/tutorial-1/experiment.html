Quick concepts

Membrane basics

1. Gradients

K⁺ is high inside the cell, while Na⁺ and Cl⁻ are higher outside.

2. Equilibrium

Each ion has its own voltage where electrical and chemical forces balance.

3. Permeability

The membrane potential moves toward the ion that is most permeable.

Vm ≈ 61.5 × log10[(PK·Kout + PNa·Naout + PCl·Clin) / (PK·Kin + PNa·Nain + PCl·Clout)]
