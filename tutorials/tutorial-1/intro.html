Tutorial 1: Resting Membrane Potential & the GHK Equation

Welcome!

In this tutorial, you'll explore one of the most fundamental concepts in neurophysiology: how cells maintain a resting membrane potential and what determines its value.

By the end of this tutorial, you will understand that:

  • The membrane potential is determined by ion permeability and concentration gradients
  • Different ions have different equilibrium potentials
  • The GHK equation mathematically describes how these factors combine
  • Changes in ion concentrations and permeabilities can dramatically affect neural excitability

What You'll Learn

📊

Equilibrium Potentials

Understand why each ion "wants" to push the membrane to a specific voltage.

⚗️

The GHK Equation

Learn the equation that describes how permeability and concentration determine Vm.

🧪

Interactive Experiments

Use simulations to test hypotheses about how ions affect membrane potential.

🎯

Clinical Relevance

See how imbalances in ion concentrations cause medical problems like hyperkalemia.

Tour of the Interface

The simulator you'll use has three main components:

1

Control Panel

On the left, you'll find sliders to adjust:

  • Extracellular ion concentrations (what's outside the cell)
  • Membrane permeability (how easily ions can cross)

Note: We'll use a glial cell, which is primarily permeable to K⁺ ions

2

Real-Time Display

In the center, you'll see:

  • Vm (Membrane Potential) - the actual voltage across the membrane
  • Equilibrium Potentials - where each ion "wants" to push the voltage
  • Current ion concentrations
3

Graph

On the right, a live graph shows how Vm changes as you adjust parameters. This helps you visualize the relationship between variables.
